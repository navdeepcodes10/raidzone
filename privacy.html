Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 13, 2026 • Official Platform Document

Welcome to Raid Zone ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you use our mobile application and website.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect data strictly necessary to deliver a verified Kabaddi tournament discovery experience:

  • Guest Users: You can explore tournaments, filter weight categories, view match posters, and get ground directions completely anonymously without creating an account or submitting any personal info.
  • Tournament Organisers: When you register as an organiser to publish events, we securely collect your name, email address, phone/WhatsApp contact number, and sports committee credentials.
  • Uploaded Materials: Tournament posters, prize amounts, venue locations, and match rules uploaded by registered organisers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To display authentic tournament listings and organiser contact lines to players and teams.
  • To prevent duplicate, cancelled, or deceptive tournament submissions.
  • To verify authentic sports committees with our verified blue badge.
  • To communicate crucial listing updates or moderation decisions.

3. Data Protection & Zero Third-Party Monetization

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Information is only shared under the following conditions:

  • Public Match Info: Organiser contact numbers and tournament posters that you explicitly publish are displayed publicly so players can call or WhatsApp to confirm registrations.
  • Secure Infrastructure: We use Supabase cloud infrastructure with Row Level Security (RLS) and encrypted HTTPS connections to protect authentication records.

4. Account & Complete Data Deletion

You retain complete ownership over your data. Tournament organisers can permanently delete their account and all uploaded posters directly inside the Raid Zone mobile application:

  • Open Raid Zone → Go to Settings → Tap Delete Account Permanently.
  • Upon confirmation, your profile, authentication records, and uploaded posters are permanently purged from our database and storage servers.
  • You may also request manual account deletion anytime by emailing support@raidzone.in or contacting our admin via WhatsApp at +91 8708444708.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to:

  • Email: support@raidzone.in
  • WhatsApp Support: +91 8708444708
  • Platform: Raid Zone — Kabaddi Tournament Discovery
