Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Installation?

Here are quick answers to common questions about direct app installation.

Why does Android show "File might be harmful"?
Android shows this standard security warning whenever an app is downloaded directly from a web browser instead of the Google Play Store (this is the same notice shown when downloading Dream11, MPL, or WhatsApp directly). Raid Zone is 100% clean, verified, and free of any harmful code. Tap "Download anyway" to proceed safely.
Do I need to sign in with Google or create an account?
No account is needed to browse tournaments, view match posters, filter weight categories, or get ground directions. You only need an account if you are a tournament organiser publishing your own tournament.
Which Android phones can run Raid Zone?
Raid Zone runs smoothly on all Android phones running Android 7.0 (Nougat) or newer (released from 2016 onward). It has been tested and verified on Poco, Xiaomi, Redmi, Samsung, Realme, Vivo, OnePlus, and Motorola devices.
